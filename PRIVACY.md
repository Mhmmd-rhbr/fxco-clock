# Privacy policy — AryamerX Financial Clock

Effective date: 16 November 2026  
Applies to extension version: 3.0.0

AryamerX Financial Clock is a free tool for viewing reference market sessions and planning personal time around them. Its core functions run in the browser without an account, a subscription, or a connection to a broker.

## Information stored by the extension

Version 3 stores its settings and user-created content in `chrome.storage.local`, within the browser profile. Depending on the features you use, this can include your preferred language, theme, time zone, session selections, pair presets, reminder rules, quiet hours, personal availability, custom broker/session schedules, and preparation checklists or notes. The extension also stores small operational records, such as whether an old settings migration has completed and which reminders have already been handled, to avoid repeated notifications. This data is not encrypted by the extension; people with access to your browser profile may be able to read it. Avoid entering sensitive account or financial information.

The extension does not ask for or collect a name, email address, trading-account credentials, broker password, payment information, browsing history, cookies, or the contents of the websites you visit. It does not connect to a trading account or execute trades.

## Network access and third parties

The extension does not send your settings or content to AryamerX or to an analytics service. It does not make background requests for market prices, economic calendars, advertising, remote fonts, remote scripts, or AI services. Runtime code, interface assets, and reference session definitions are included in the extension package.

Links to AryamerX or reference sources open only when you choose to open them. Visiting an external website is a separate interaction with that website, which may receive normal web-request information and apply its own privacy policy. The extension does not attach your saved settings or notes to these links.

## Browser permissions

- **Storage** saves your settings and personal planning data in the browser profile.
- **Alarms** schedules local reminder and toolbar updates while the extension popup is closed. It does not make the browser or computer run when they are shut down.
- **Side panel** displays the extension alongside the current website. It does not grant the extension access to the website's contents.
- **Notifications** is optional. The extension requests it when you choose to enable desktop reminders. You can turn reminders off or revoke notification permission in Chrome's extension settings.

The extension does not require permission to read every website, your history, cookies, or trading-platform content.

## Migration from older versions

Older versions may have saved preferences using `chrome.storage.sync`, which Chrome can synchronize through your Google account when Chrome Sync is enabled. Version 3 can read those legacy preferences once to preserve supported settings, and then saves version 3 data locally. Version 3 does not write new settings or personal content to Chrome Sync.

Migrating or removing local version 3 data does not necessarily remove an older synchronized copy retained by Chrome. Chrome Sync is controlled by Chrome and your Google-account settings, rather than by an AryamerX server.

## Exports, clipboard, and reminders

Calendar files and copied time summaries are generated locally when you request them. If you import a calendar file into another app, save an exported file elsewhere, or paste copied text into a service, that destination is controlled by you and may have its own data practices.

Desktop notifications can show session names and reminder times to people who can see your screen. Chrome and your operating system control whether notifications are displayed and whether they remain in notification history. Notification delivery can be delayed by sleep, browser closure, operating-system settings, or power management.

## Your controls and retention

You can edit or remove your saved plans, reminders, custom schedules, and preparation notes using the extension's controls. Settings → **Erase local data** resets version 3 preferences, plans, notes, custom schedules, and local reminder records. A migration-complete marker is retained so erased settings are not automatically imported again from an older Chrome Sync copy. You can also remove the extension through Chrome to remove its browser-profile data. Exported files, copied text, operating-system notification history, and legacy Chrome Sync copies are separate and are not removed by the extension's local controls.

AryamerX does not receive a server-side copy of your local extension data, so it cannot recover that data for you or delete copies you have saved in other applications.

## Time and session information

Clocks and schedules depend on your device clock and the time-zone rules supplied by your browser. Bundled reference session conventions describe general planning windows. They are not a live feed of broker availability, prices, liquidity, exchange holidays, or guaranteed execution hours. Custom schedules are values entered by you.

## Policy changes and contact

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy and the relevant store disclosures must be updated to match the released behavior. For support or privacy questions, use the contact options on the [AryamerX website](https://aryamerx.com/extensions).

اعلان دسکتاپ اختیاری است. پیوند سایت یا منبع فقط با انتخاب شما باز می‌شود. فایل تقویم و متن قابل کپی نیز به درخواست شما و به‌صورت محلی ساخته می‌شوند. پس از انتقال فایل یا متن به برنامه دیگر، شرایط همان برنامه اعمال می‌شود.

برای حفظ تنظیمات، نسخه ۳ ممکن است یک بار تنظیمات نسخه قدیمی را از Chrome Sync بخواند؛ داده‌های تازه نسخه ۳ در فضای محلی ذخیره می‌شوند. نسخه قدیمی همگام‌شده ممکن است همچنان در حساب Chrome باقی مانده باشد و حذف داده محلی لزوماً آن نسخه را پاک نمی‌کند.

ساعت‌ها به ساعت دستگاه و قواعد منطقه زمانی مرورگر وابسته‌اند. برنامه مرجع سشن‌ها اطلاعات زمان‌بندی است و وضعیت واقعی بروکر، تعطیلی کامل بازار یا نقدشوندگی لحظه‌ای را تضمین نمی‌کند. بستن مرورگر، خاموشی یا خواب دستگاه و تنظیمات سیستم می‌توانند اعلان‌ها را به تأخیر بیندازند.
